# Glossary

## Incident
An emergency event initiated by a person in need via chat.

## Volunteer
A registered responder who has completed the chat onboarding and has a Reputation/Availability score (baseline 50/100).

## Dispatch
The automated process of selecting the top 3 volunteers for an incident based on distance and hardcoded weights.

## SuperAdmin
A hardcoded staff account with the authority to approve new staff members who sign in via OAuth.

## Verification Gate
A concrete check proving that a phase is complete in `TASKS.md`.
